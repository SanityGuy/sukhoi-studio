The Flanker Family

The Sukhoi Su-27 Flanker is a twin-engine, supermaneuverable air superiority fighter developed by the Sukhoi Design Bureau during the Cold War. Designed in response to the United States Air Force's McDonnell Douglas F-15 Eagle, the Su-27 entered service with the Soviet Air Forces in 1985 and became one of the most capable fourth-generation fighters ever built.

The Su-27 formed the foundation of the renowned Flanker family, which later expanded into specialized variants including the multirole Su-30, carrier-based Su-33, strike-focused Su-34, highly modernized Su-35S, and ultimately the fifth-generation Su-57 Felon.
