Deep Strike & Multirole Dominance

The Sukhoi Su-30 (NATO reporting name: Flanker-C) is a two-seat, twin-engine fighter aircraft developed by Russia's Sukhoi Aviation Corporation. It is a multirole fighter built to execute deep interdiction missions alongside air superiority operations.

The aircraft performed its maiden flight on 31 December 1989. With an integrated second crew member functioning as a Weapons System Officer (WSO), the Su-30 can simultaneously manage complex air-to-ground guided munitions while the pilot focuses on flight and air-to-air threats. It serves as a backbone fighter for numerous air forces globally.

Avionics Systems

System Description
N011M Bars Radar A passive electronically scanned array (PESA) radar providing advanced multi-target tracking and ground-mapping capabilities (equipped on advanced export and SM variants).
OLS-30 Upgraded optical-locator system with IRST and laser rangefinder functionality for silent targeting and precision ground-strike designation.
Khibiny Electronic Warfare Suite Advanced self-protection jammer and electronic countermeasures system designed to disrupt enemy missile tracking and radar systems.
WSO Tactical Suite The rear-seat station is equipped with dedicated tactical displays allowing the Weapons System Officer to manage complex guided munitions and radar data during combat.
