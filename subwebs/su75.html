Light Tactical Aviation & Open Architecture

The Sukhoi Su-75 Checkmate (LTS – Light Tactical Aircraft) is a single-engine, fifth-generation stealth fighter concept unveiled by Sukhoi and Rostec at the MAKS-2021 airshow. Designed as a cost-effective counterpart to heavy twin-engine fighters like the Su-57, the Checkmate places a strong focus on low radar visibility and artificial intelligence support.

Featuring a signature Diverterless Supersonic Inlet (DSI), ruddervators in a V-tail arrangement, and wide internal weapon bays, the Su-75 is built with an open-architecture configuration allowing easy customization for foreign air forces, as well as uncrewed or optionally manned deployment.

Avionics & Modern Innovations

System Description
AESA Radar System Active Electronically Scanned Array radar designed for tracking multiple air and ground targets simultaneously under strong electronic jamming.
AI Flight Assistant Integrated artificial intelligence co-pilot that assists with threat analysis, combat prioritizing, and automating routine operational tasks.
Matreshka Support System Automated ground diagnostic logistics platform utilizing predictive analytics to streamline maintenance schedules and lower operating costs.
Internal Weapon Bays Main central bay and side bays housing RVV-MD2 short-range, RVV-SD medium-range missiles, and precision-guided air-to-surface weapons.
Unmanned Capability Airframe designed to be built in fully autonomous or uncrewed combat air vehicle (UCAV) configurations without major structural redesigns.
