SU-57 FELON

Russia's first operational fifth-generation multirole fighter, combining reduced radar observability, advanced sensors, and exceptional maneuverability.

Search for specifications

Stealth & Supercruise

The Sukhoi Su-57 (NATO reporting name: Felon) is a twin-engine, fifth-generation multirole fighter developed under Russia's PAK FA (Prospective Airborne Complex of Frontline Aviation) program. The program was launched in the early 2000s to replace aging fourth-generation fighters such as the Su-27 while complementing aircraft including the MiG-29.

The aircraft performed its maiden flight on 29 January 2010. It combines reduced radar observability, supermaneuverability, advanced sensor fusion, internal weapons carriage, and the ability to conduct both air superiority and precision strike missions. Unlike many stealth aircraft, the Su-57 places a strong emphasis on kinematic performance alongside low observability.

Avionics Systems

System Description
N036 Byelka AESA Radar Distributed active electronically scanned array radar system consisting of a nose-mounted X-band array and additional side-looking arrays, providing wide target coverage and improved situational awareness.
101KS "Atoll" Integrated electro-optical system incorporating infrared search and track (IRST), missile approach warning sensors, laser warning receivers, and directional infrared countermeasures (DIRCM).
L402 Himalayas Electronic warfare suite designed for radar warning, electronic attack, self-protection, and countermeasures against modern air defense systems.
Glass Cockpit Digital cockpit featuring large multifunction displays, wide-angle head-up display, HOTAS controls, and advanced mission computers.
Internal Weapons Bays Two in each central weapons bays for beyond-visual-range missiles and guided munitions, plus two side bays for short-range air-to-air missiles while maintaining reduced radar signature.
