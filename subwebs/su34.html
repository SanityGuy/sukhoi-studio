SU-34 FULLBACK

Russia's premier twin-engine, twin-seat, all-weather supersonic medium-range fighter-bomber and tactical strike aircraft.

Search for specifications

Tactical Bombing & Strike Capabilities

The Sukhoi Su-34 (NATO reporting name: Fullback) is heavily derived from the Su-27 Flanker airframe but features a radically redesigned "platypus" nose section to accommodate an advanced terrain-following radar and a side-by-side armored cockpit.

The aircraft performed its maiden flight on 13 April 1990. Designed to replace the Su-24 variable-sweep wing strike fighter, the Su-34 possesses the heavy payload capability of a dedicated bomber while retaining enough agility and speed to defend itself against opposing fighter threats.

Avionics Systems

System Description
Leninets V004 Radar A passive electronically scanned array (PESA) radar optimized for terrain-following operations and advanced ground-strike targeting, housed in the distinctive "platypus" nose.
Platan Electro-Optical System A retractable laser/TV targeting system used for guiding precision air-to-surface munitions independently without the need for external targeting pods.
Khibiny-10V Integrated electronic warfare suite usually housed in wingtip pods, providing comprehensive self-protection against ground-to-air and air-to-air radar threats.
Armored Cockpit The entire side-by-side cockpit is enclosed in a titanium armored tub, providing substantial protection for the crew against small arms and anti-aircraft artillery during low-level strikes.
