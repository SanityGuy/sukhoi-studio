Air Superiority & Super-Maneuverability

The Sukhoi Su-35 (NATO reporting name: Flanker-E) is a single-seat, twin-engine deeply modernized 4++ generation multirole air-superiority fighter. Designed as an interim platform bridging legacy fourth-generation fighters with fifth-generation technologies, the Su-35 eliminates the need for airbrakes by utilizing automated control surfaces and thrust vectoring.

The aircraft performed its maiden flight on 19 February 2008. Powered by twin Saturn AL-41F1S turbofans featuring independently canted 3D thrust-vectoring nozzles, the Su-35 achieves exceptional high angle-of-attack maneuverability, executing post-stall maneuvers such as Pugachev's Cobra, the Frolov Chakra (Kulbit), and post-stall pitch reversals.

Service Ceiling (max.) 18,000 m (59,000 ft)
Ferry Range Approximately 4,500 km (with external drop tanks)
Payload / Hardpoints 12 external hardpoints (up to 8,000 kg payload capacity)
Maximum Takeoff Weight Approximately 34,500 kg
Thrust Vectoring 3D thrust-vectoring nozzles
Supercruise Limited supersonic speed without afterburner
Operational Status In active service

* The AL-41F1S engine is a derivative of the AL-31F with increased thrust and integrated 3D thrust-vectoring nozzles, later paving the way for the Su-57 propulsion system.

Avionics & Sensor Suite

System Description
Irbis-E PESA Radar N035 Irbis-E passive electronically scanned array radar, capable of detecting air targets up to 400 km away and tracking 30 targets while simultaneously engaging 8.
OLS-35 Optoelectronic Suite Nose-mounted infrared search and track (IRST) system incorporating an optical sight, thermal imaging detector, and laser rangefinder/target designator.
Khibiny-M Suite Wingtip-mounted L175M Khibiny-M electronic warfare complex providing radar warning receiver coverage, threat identification, and active radio-jamming countermeasures.
Glass Cockpit Layout Modern glass cockpit featuring two 15-inch MFI-35 multi-function color LCD displays, a wide-angle head-up display (IKSH-1M), and integrated hands-on-throttle-and-stick (HOTAS) controls.
Heavy Armament Loadout Capable of launching R-77-1 and ultra-long-range R-37M air-to-air missiles, alongside anti-radiation (Kh-31P) and precision-guided anti-ship strike munitions.
