Air Superiority & Maneuverability

The Sukhoi Su-27 (NATO reporting name: Flanker) is a Soviet-origin twin-engine supermaneuverable fighter aircraft designed by Sukhoi. It was intended as a direct competitor for the large United States fourth-generation fighters such as the F-15 Eagle, with 3,530-kilometre range, heavy aircraft ordnance, sophisticated avionics and high maneuverability.

The aircraft performed its maiden flight on 20 May 1977. It is designed for air superiority missions, but subsequent variants are able to perform almost all aerial warfare operations. The Su-27 became the foundational airframe for a highly successful family of aircraft, setting the standard for aerodynamic design and combat capability in the late 20th century.

Avionics Systems

System Description
Phazotron N001 Myech Radar A pulse-Doppler radar capable of tracking multiple targets, though primarily focused on air-to-air engagements and beyond-visual-range combat.
OEPS-27 IRST Electro-optical targeting system combining an infrared search and track (IRST) sensor with a laser rangefinder for passive target acquisition.
SPO-15 Sirena Radar warning receiver system providing the pilot with situational awareness of hostile radar locks and threats.
Helmet Mounted Sight (HMS) Allows the pilot to lock on to targets simply by looking at them, highly effective when paired with high-off-boresight missiles like the R-73.
